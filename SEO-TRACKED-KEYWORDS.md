Created 2026-09-09. This file is the **owned** copy of what we set up in Semrush
position tracking. Semrush is a rented measurement layer; the targets and the
reasoning below are ours and must survive the subscription being cancelled.

Semrush account `lronealgorithm@gmail.com` (ID 27041356). Both campaigns:
Google, Desktop, United States, weekly email updates on.

## Why this file exists

If the Semrush subscription lapses we lose the *rank history it computed*, and
that history cannot be rebuilt afterwards. What we must not also lose is the
target list and the reasoning for it, because that is the actual strategy.

The durable hedge is **Google Search Console**, not this file. Search Console
gives us query-level impressions, clicks and average position for free, for as
long as we own the domain, and we can export it. Semrush adds competitor
context, difficulty scores and daily granularity on top. Treat Semrush as the
enrichment layer and Search Console as the system of record.
