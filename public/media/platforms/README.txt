================================================================================
on-dark/  — REVERSED VARIANTS FOR THE FOOTER (added 2026-08-30)
================================================================================

The footer sits on #04182b. Rendering all 31 marks on that ground showed 17 with
ink that disappeared into it -- not only the all-black marks (GitHub, OpenAI,
Grok, TikTok) but the dark wordmark half of Claude, Cloudflare, LinkedIn, Meta,
monday.com, QuickBooks, ServiceNow, Shopify, HubSpot, Dynamics, DocuSign, Ghost
and Zendesk.

on-dark/ holds a reversed twin of every mark, generated by ONE rule:

    a colour becomes white only if it is BOTH
      (a) invisible on the ground   -- contrast < 3:1 against #04182b, and
      (b) a dark neutral            -- lightness < 0.35
    everything else is left exactly as the owner drew it.

That is what an official reversed lockup does. LinkedIn keeps its blue box and
the white "in"; Cloudflare keeps its orange cloud; ServiceNow keeps its green
"o"; Claude keeps its orange sunburst. No brand colour is lost, so this is not
the recolouring forbidden above -- it is the dark-background form of the mark.
DocuSign's #4c00ff stays violet because at lightness 0.50 it is a saturated
brand colour, not dark neutral ink.

⛔ notion.svg is NOT in on-dark/. Its black N and rule sit on the logo's own
white page, so it is already legible on navy; whitening it merges the N into the
page and yields a solid white blob. Any future mark carrying its own light
ground belongs in the same exception -- see ownGround in
client/components/TrustedPartnerships.tsx.

⛔ Use the originals in this folder for LIGHT grounds, on-dark/ for the footer.
Do not delete the originals: they are the unmodified marks.
